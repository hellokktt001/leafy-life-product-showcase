Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to receive subscription products?

Products are typically shipped within 3-5 business days after your subscription is processed, with delivery times depending on your location.

How do I cancel or modify my subscription?

You can log into your account and change or cancel your subscription on the "My Subscriptions" page. Alternatively, you can email our customer service, and we'll process your request within 24 hours.

What if I receive damaged plants?

Please take photos and contact our customer service within 24 hours of receiving your package, and we'll arrange for a replacement or refund immediately.

Do you offer customized plant subscription services?

Yes, we offer customization options. You can fill out a plant preference form when subscribing or contact our customer service team to discuss your specific requirements.
